"§4 · LAYER 1\nCurrency Constituency Engine\nadaptive membership · hysteresis-gated\n\nThe Constituency Engine is Layer 1 of the three-layer architecture (§2.2). MTQΣ maintains an eligible universe and independently decides which currencies qualify for the active basket. Membership is an algorithmic property with hysteresis, so the system can admit a rising currency or retire a deteriorating one without discretionary intervention and without thrashing at the boundary. The engine is evaluated on the strategic review cadence (quarterly by default, §10.10) and on structural-change events. On this pilot, admission remains governed — the universe below is validation-stage; no membership change has ever been executed.\n\n§4.2 · ELIGIBILITY SCORING\nThe Eligibility Score Q_i\n8 components · 6 positive · 2 negative\n\nFor every candidate currency i, the engine computes a composite eligibility score from six positive and two negative components. A currency becomes eligible for the active basket only if Q_i ≥ Q_min.\n\nQ_i = a_R·R_i + a_T·T_i + a_L·L_i + a_S·S_i + a_D·D_i + a_C·C_i − a_G·G_i − a_X·X_i\nComponent\tSymbol\tSign\tMeasured property\nReserve relevance\tR_i\t+ positive\tShare and persistence of currency i in global official reserves (IMF COFER).\nTrade / payment relevance\tT_i\t+ positive\tInternational trade invoicing, payment usage and cross-border settlement volume.\nMarket liquidity\tL_i\t+ positive\tFX turnover, market depth, bid-ask spread quality, settlement availability.\nStability characteristics\tS_i\t+ positive\tMulti-horizon volatility and purchasing-power stability (Section 5.1).\nDiversification contribution\tD_i\t+ positive\tMarginal risk reduction contributed to the basket portfolio.\nConvertibility / depth\tC_i\t+ positive\tConvertibility, market depth and transferability of the currency.\nGeopolitical concentration risk\tG_i\t− negative\tConcentration of issuing jurisdiction in sanctions, freeze or conflict exposure.\nCapital-control / transferability risk\tX_i\t− negative\tRisk of capital controls or transferability restrictions.\nScore normalization — §4.2.1\n\nRaw components are measured in different units (reserve shares in percent, turnover in trillions, volatility in annualized decimal), so Q_i is normalized before threshold comparison — otherwise datasets with different units silently distort the score [COO-1]. Default: min–max across the candidate universe; a percentile or rank transformation is an approved bounded alternative for heavy-tailed distributions or small universes. Whichever transform is selected becomes part of the published methodology version (§24.1).\n\nQ̃_i = (Q_i − Q_min) / (Q_max − Q_min)\n\nThe coefficients a_k and the threshold Q_min are methodology parameters — fixed per published methodology version; any change requires Risk Council approval through the parameter-registry process (§22.3). The coefficients are themselves subject to methodology validation (§23.6), and the entire scoring table is published with each weight publication (§24.3) so eligibility decisions remain reproducible.\n\nStructural score construction — §4.5\n\nEvery input is normalized before aggregation [MS §8]. The default transform is the z-score, where μ_k and σ_k are the cross-sectional mean and standard deviation of input k across the candidate universe:\n\nz_i,k = (x_i,k − μ_k) / σ_k\n\nA bounded percentile or rank transform is substituted when distributional stability is preferred — when an input is heavy-tailed or the universe is small enough that a single outlier would dominate the z-score. The composite structural score is then:\n\nS_i^Structural = ∑_k a_k · z_i,k\n\nThe aggregation weights a_k are themselves subject to methodology validation: the sensitivity of the final basket to each a_k is tested by parameter perturbation (§23.6), and unstable coefficients are re-derived or the input is re-transformed. This closes the loop — the constituency engine is as auditable as the weighting engine, because every intermediate score is published with the weights (§24.3). Pilot honesty: the Q_i model is validation-stage (spec-plus-live-COFER inputs); membership changes have never been executed.\n\n§4.3 · INSTITUTIONAL DATA\nInstitutional Data Sources\ninputs — never weights\nIMF COFER — reserve relevance (R_i)\n\nThe Currency Composition of Official Foreign Exchange Reserves (COFER) is the primary reserve-relevance input. "§24 · PUBLICATION\nTransparency, Reproducibility & Publication\ndeterministic · versioned · public\nTESTNET ONLY\n·\neverything published on this page is pilot output\n\nThe publication machinery below — version tuples, weight series, decision records — is exercised live on the testnet pilot so its determinism can be inspected.
