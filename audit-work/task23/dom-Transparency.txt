The production publication layer requires the §25.5 validation gates, which have not been passed.\n\n§24.1 · DETERMINISM\nDeterministic Reproducibility\nG7 gate prerequisite\n\nThe transparency architecture rests on a single computational fact: given identical inputs, the system must produce identical outputs —\n\n(Data_t, Parameters_t, Versions_t) identical ⟹ W_t^Live identical\n\nThe MASE registry intentionally does not re-solve the optimization on-chain; that omission is safe only because anyone can re-run MASE off-chain from the published versions and obtain the identical target, which the contract then verifies against the constitution. The auditor is not asked to trust the submitter — only to execute the same deterministic function on the same versioned inputs [MS §92].\n\n• Random generators seeded with published constants (MC families reproduce path-for-path, §23.5).\n• Floating-point operations in a fixed order and precision.\n• No computation depends on wall-clock time, network latency or environment state.\n• Software versions recorded; input data snapshotted immutably under its DataVersion.\n\nA re-run that diverges in even one weight at one timestamp is a defect that blocks the G7 reproducibility gate of §23.12.\n\n§24 · §34\nDeterministic Reproducibility\n\nRe-run the calculation from the same inputs and verify the output matches\n\nDATA VERSION\nLive (Frankfurter ECB + gold-api + Yahoo ^VIX + Yahoo DX-Y.NYB (or Frankfurter self-calc))\nMODEL VERSION\n8term-v2-pilot-uniform-lambda-plus-5.5-regime-scaled-cvar\nPARAM VERSION\nBLUEPRINT-v2.0 · §22.4 registry (pilot)\nORACLE VERSION\n3-source board · aggregate NORMAL\nREPRODUCE\n\nVersion lineage above is live (same fields as the §24.2 cards in the Transparency section). The target is an unconstrained-in-spirit optimum: what stability mathematics wants, before the constitution speaks.\n\nSTEP 2 · CONSTRAINT\nW_t^Constrained = Projection_Ct( W_t^Target )\n\nChapter 8: the constitutional envelopes, sum-to-one and non-negativity, the weight-velocity limits and the eligibility bounds define C_t. The projection is applied after the ensemble average (§7.5), because an average of admissible solutions is not automatically admissible itself. This is where the constitution has the final word over the mathematics.\n\nSTEP 3 · SMOOTHING\nW_t^Smooth = ρ_t · W_{t−1} + (1 − ρ_t) · W_t^Constrained\n\nThe §8.4 stress-adaptive rule: ρ_t = 0.50 in normal conditions, rising to 0.75 when the crisis score is elevated — the basket moves deliberately in calm markets and even more deliberately in turbulent ones. With the velocity limits and the direction lock (§11.5) this implements stability-in-time; W^Target ≠ W^Smooth is one of the four weight states of §2.3.\n\nSTEP 4 · EXECUTION\nW_t^Execution = MARP( W_t^Smooth, W_t^Actual, Cost_t, Liquidity_t, Reserve_t )\n\nChapters 10–11: MARP compares the smoothed target with the actual weights, applies the no-trade thresholds and the cost-benefit test, scales and times execution against liquidity and cost, and respects the reserve state — producing what actually trades, logged field-by-field under its DecisionID (§24.4). W_t^Execution becomes the actual weight vector of the next period, closing the loop.\n\nThe final equation is the audit trail in miniature: Data_t (§24.2 versioning) through Constituency and Ensemble (Ch. 4; Ch. 5–7), through Constrain and Smooth (Ch. 8), to Execute (Ch. 10–11) — with every state transition published (§24.3) and every executed movement logged (§24.4). Validation (Ch. 23) establishes that the pipeline deserves to run; transparency (this chapter) establishes that its running can always be checked."
