  1165→definition of the unit is a methodology rather than a fixed quantity
  1166→table — which is precisely why the methodology must itself be
  1167→constitutionally fixed (Invariant I3, Section 2.6).

  1231→                                      target.           
  1232→
  1233→  Execution         W^{Execution}     Actual trade      MARP (Chapters
  1234→                                      decision after    10–11).
  1235→                                      cost, liquidity

  1240→W^(StrategicPrior) ≠ W^(Target) ≠ W^(Smoothed) ≠ W^(Execution)
  1241→
  1242→These four states are not equivalent, and none of them is the published

  1246→even if the target happens to equal the prior for many days, the system
  1247→state remains adaptive because the path from data to decision is what is
  1248→constitutional, not the destination.

  1250→2.4 The Two-System Architecture: Reference Basket vs Reserve
  1251→
  1252→Within the constitutional separation, MTQ Σ consists of two
  1253→mathematically related but operationally separate systems [MS §1]. The
  1254→Reference Basket defines what one unit of MTQ Σ represents; the Reserve
  1255→Architecture determines which eligible assets are actually held to
  1256→satisfy MTQ Σ obligations, liquidity and collateral requirements. Their
  1257→compositions are deliberately decoupled:
  1258→
  1259→W^(Index) ≠ W^(Reserve)
  1260→
  1261→in general. A 27% USD reference weight does not obligate the reserve to
  1262→hold 27% USD-denominated assets; the reserve is sized by redemption
  1263→risk, liquidity and collateral requirements (Chapter 13). The initial
  1264→candidate universe of the reference basket is:
  1265→
  1266→U = {USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, CNY, CHF, CAD, AUD, SGD, …, GOLD}

  1778→3. The Adaptive Reference Basket
  1779→
  1780→3.1 The No-Fixed-Weighting Principle
  1781→
  1782→  MTQ Σ must not be defined as a fixed percentage table. The single most
  1783→  important architectural rule of v2.0 is that composition is an output
  1784→  of transparent mathematics, never an input [MS §2].
  1785→
  1786→A statement such as the one below is categorically not a definition of
  1787→MTQ Σ:
  1788→
  1789→MTQ Σ ≠ { USD=27%, EUR=20%, JPY=9%, GBP=8%, CNY=5%, CHF=5%, Gold=26% }
  1790→
  1791→Those numbers are only W^{StrategicPrior} — starting priors used to
  1792→stabilize the optimization process and to provide a transparent initial
  1793→reference. They are soft anchors subject to a deviation penalty (Section
  1794→7.1), not targets to be hit. The live system computes:
  1795→
  1796→W_(t)^(Live) = MASE(Data_(t), W^(Prior), Risk_(t), Macro_(t), PP_(t),
  1797→Liquidity_(t), Cost_(t))
  1798→
  1799→and therefore, in general:
  1800→
  1801→W_(t)^(Live) ≠ W^(StrategicPrior)
  1802→
  1803→Three consequences follow. First, any communication that quotes a
  1804→percentage as the composition of MTQ Σ is definitionally wrong; only the
  1805→published W_t series (Section 24.3) describes composition. Second, the
  1806→strategic prior table is versioned research data — if the research
  1807→program revises the prior, the change flows through governance as a new
  1808→methodology version, not as a silent redefinition. Third, stability of
  1809→the unit is achieved by bounding how fast weights may move (Chapter 8)
  1810→and by the smoothing and execution gates (Chapters 8 and 10), not by
  1811→freezing the weights themselves.
  1812→

  1814→
  1815→The current research prior — the starting point for the optimizer and
  1816→the genesis snapshot — is [MS §3]:
  1817→
  1818→  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  1819→  Component                           Initial Strategic Prior
  1820→  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  1821→  USD                                 27%
  1822→
  1823→  EUR                                 20%
  1824→
  1825→  JPY                                 9%
  1826→
  1827→  GBP                                 8%
  1828→
  1829→  CNY                                 5%
  1830→
  1831→  CHF                                 5%
  1832→
  1833→  Gold                                26%
  1834→

  1842→
  1843→That inequality is deliberately repeated directly beneath the prior
  1844→table, because it is the single most important correction in the entire
  1845→architecture [COO Review, opening item]. The published starting basket
  1846→above is W^{Prior} — a research input and a soft anchor. It is
  1847→categorically not W_t, the live weight vector: the live system computes
  1848→W_t^{Target} = MASE(Data_t) and then applies constraints, smoothing and
  1849→execution testing before any weight becomes effective (the four states
  1850→are defined in §2.3). One equation, placed exactly where the numbers are
  1851→quoted, prevents the most damaging future misreading — the accidental
  1852→recreation of a fixed-weight basket in the mind of a reader, a regulator
  1853→or a marketing page.
  1854→
  1855→The prior was derived from the intersection of reserve relevance, trade
  1856→and payment usage, liquidity depth and diversification contribution
  1857→across the candidate universe; the derivation data are published with
  1858→the methodology version. The prior enters the MASE objective only
  1859→through the StrategicDrift penalty term (Section 7.1), so it influences
  1860→but never dictates. If validation (Chapter 23) shows that a different
  1861→prior produces a more robust optimum, the prior itself is re-derived and
  1862→re-published — the definition of the unit never wavers because the unit
  1863→is the methodology.
  1864→
  1865→3.3 Gold as a First-Class Reference-Basket Component
  1866→
  1867→Gold is a full member of the reference basket with its own adaptive
  1868→allocation W_{Gold,t} [MS §4]. It is not a multiplier applied to
  1869→currencies and not merely a reserve asset. The historical architecture
  1870→used a gold factor of the form:
  1871→
  1872→W_(i,raw) = S_(i) · K_(i)
  1873→
  1874→where the gold factor modified individual currency weights. Under v2.0
  1875→that mechanism is retained only as one input signal to the new engine;
  1876→gold itself now receives an independent adaptive allocation. Gold's
  1877→target allocation is a function of its own characteristics [MS §14]:
  1878→
  1879→W_(G,t)^(Target) = f(StrategicPrior_(G), RiskContribution_(G),
  1880→Diversification_(G), Inflation_(G), Crisis_(G), Liquidity_(G),
  1881→PurchasingPower_(G))
  1882→
  1883→rather than a constant. Gold remains strategically valuable because its
  1884→diversification behavior can become particularly useful under stress —
  1885→gold correlations to fiat currencies tend to shift in crisis regimes —
  1886→but those correlations are dynamic rather than permanently fixed, which
  1887→is exactly why the allocation must be adaptive rather than
  1888→constitutional. The gold weight bounds in Section 8.1 (20–32% envelope)
  1889→are validation-stage admissibility ranges for this component, not
  1890→weighting targets.

  1946→3.5 Basket Size and Unit Economics
  1947→
  1948→One unit of MTQ Σ represents one unit of the reference index I_t, which
  1949→is normalized to 1.00 at genesis. The denomination is a normalization
  1950→choice, not a price promise: the denomination does not imply that MTQ Σ
  1951→remains at a fixed market price, because the index — a weighted basket
  1952→of currencies and gold — moves with its constituents. Users minting at
  1953→index value 1.12 receive fewer tokens per USD than users minting at
  1954→0.92, which is the purchasing-power-equivalence behavior specified in
  1955→Chapter 19.

  1957→4. The Currency Constituency Engine
  1958→
  1959→4.1 Purpose and Design
  1960→
  1961→The Constituency Engine is Layer 1 of the three-layer architecture
  1962→(Section 2.2). MTQ Σ maintains an eligible universe and independently
  1963→decides which currencies qualify for the active basket [MS §5].
  1964→Membership is an algorithmic property with hysteresis, so the system can
  1965→admit a rising currency (for example, a future reserve-eligible issuer)
  1966→or retire a deteriorating one without discretionary intervention and
  1967→without thrashing at the boundary. The engine is evaluated on the
  1968→strategic review cadence (quarterly by default; see Section 10.10) and
  1969→on structural-change events.
