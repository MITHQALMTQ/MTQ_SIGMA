   853→- The task said "In computeSnapshot(), after the existing calculations, add: ... Call smoothWeights(prevSmoothed, constrained) for the smoothed weights". This is satisfied — `buildMaseSnapshot` (called from `computeSnapshot`) computes smoothed weights using `s.maseSmoothed` as the prev.
