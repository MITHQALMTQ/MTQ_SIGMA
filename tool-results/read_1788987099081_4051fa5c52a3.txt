  1250→2.4 The Two-System Architecture: Reference Basket vs Reserve
  1251→
  1252→Within the constitutional separation, MTQ Σ consists of two
  1253→mathematically related but operationally separate systems [MS §1]. The
  1254→Reference Basket defines what one unit of MTQ Σ represents; the Reserve
  1255→Architecture determines which eligible assets are actually held to
  1256→satisfy MTQ Σ obligations, liquidity and collateral requirements. Their
  1257→compositions are deliberately decoupled:
  1258→
  1259→W^(Index) ≠ W^(Reserve)
  1260→
  1261→in general. A 27% USD reference weight does not obligate the reserve to
  1262→hold 27% USD-denominated assets; the reserve is sized by redemption
  1263→risk, liquidity and collateral requirements (Chapter 13). The initial
  1264→candidate universe of the reference basket is:
  1265→
  1266→U = {USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, CNY, CHF, CAD, AUD, SGD, …, GOLD}
  1267→
  1268→The initial core candidates are \{USD, EUR, JPY, GBP, CNY, CHF, GOLD\},
  1269→but membership is not permanently fixed — the Constituency Engine
  1270→(Chapter 4) admits and retires components according to measurable
  1271→eligibility criteria with hysteresis, never by discretionary decree.
